Why Estonia, of all places?

Estonia is not a postcard diving destination — and that is the point. Below the surface it offers something rarer: a flooded Soviet-era quarry with walls and ruins to explore, dark Baltic water that rewards calm technique, and a small local team that treats every dive as training first, spectacle second.

The water here is cold, honest and quiet. Freedivers who learn in these conditions tend to carry unusual composure anywhere else they dive.

Real training. Real water. One breath.

Tallinn's old town, forest lakes and the Baltic coast make the surface half of the trip worth the flight on its own — but this site is about what waits below.

Whether you are taking a first breath-hold under supervision or bringing years of certification cards to Rummu, the local knowledge is the product: seasons, sites, conditions and honest go/no-go calls.

Guided Rummu freediving

Dive Rummu below the postcard.

A limestone quarry, a former prison, and the flooded ruins between them — Rummu is one of the most unusual freediving sites in northern Europe. It is dived with local guidance, an experience check and honest conditions calls, not as a walk-in attraction.

Explore Rummu

Four very different seasons. One year-round team.

Midsummer gives long bright evenings and the mildest water; midwinter means ice, short days and serious cold-water practice for the well-prepared. Knowing which Estonia you are flying into is half the trip.

See seasons & conditions

Depth comes after calm, technique and a buddy.

Nothing on this site asks you to be brave. Freediving here is built on relaxed breathing, unhurried technique and a buddy system — depth and duration are outcomes of calm, never goals to push for. Your first freedive is not a breath-hold contest.

Every guided dive starts with an honest conversation about experience, conditions and comfort — and the local team makes the final go/no-go call. How safety is organised in practice is described on the service site's safety section.

A small local team, not a tour operator.

freedive.ee is run by Meregrupp / Allvee Akadeemia — an Estonian freediving team that trains in local water in every season and guides visitors in English. You will be in the water with people who dive these exact sites all year, not with a franchise following a script.

Team background and credentials are published on meregrupp.ee.

Questions before you decide

Do I need any experience to start?

No. If freediving is new to you, the right first step is a guided beginner course in English — calm, structured and safety-first. Follow the Start Here route and we will point you to the course details.

Can I just show up and dive Rummu?

No — Rummu is a guided experience for people who already freedive, and every dive starts with a suitability and conditions check. Read the Rummu Quarry page to see how it works.

When is the best time to visit Estonia for freediving?

Summer is the most forgiving for a first visit, but the team trains year-round and every season has its own character. The Seasons & Conditions page walks through all four.

Is everything really in English?

Yes — training and guiding run in English, and the course and booking environment at freediving.meregrupp.ee is English-first.

Where do I see courses, dates and prices?

Course details, dates and booking live on our service site: freediving.meregrupp.ee. This site helps you choose the right route first.

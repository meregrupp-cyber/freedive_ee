Privacy

What we collect, and why.

This page describes, in plain language, what happens to information you share onfreedive.ee. If anything here is unclear, ask us at meregrupp@gmail.com.

The trip-planning form

When you send the Plan Your Trip form, we receive what you typed — name, email, optional phone, country, travel window, group size, experience level and your message — together with basic context about how you found the site (for example, which campaign link you arrived from).

We use it for one thing: answering your enquiry and planning your trip with you.

  • We reply from meregrupp@gmail.com — usually within two working days.
  • We do not sell your details or share them outside the Meregrupp team.
  • Marketing updates are sent only if you tick the separate opt-in box — it is never pre-ticked, and you can opt out any time by replying to any message.
  • Want your enquiry deleted? Email us and we will remove it.

Analytics and cookies

We want to know which pages help visitors — not who you are. Measurement on this site follows two rules:

  • No analytics or measurement cookies load unless you agree first. If no consent banner appears, no analytics is running at all.
  • Events we measure (like "a route card was clicked") never include your name, email, phone number or anything you typed into a form.

Links to our course and booking site, freediving.meregrupp.ee, may carry campaign parameters so we understand which route brought you there. Those parameters describe the link, not you.

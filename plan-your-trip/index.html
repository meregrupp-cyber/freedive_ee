Plan ahead

Plan Estonia for next season.

Looking six to twelve months ahead? Tell us your level, group size and travel window. We will help you understand the seasons, suitable training and guided-dive options before you book the trip.

Who this planning route fits

Solo travellers, couples, clubs and small groups — anyone who wants Estonia on next year's freediving calendar and would rather plan it with local knowledge than guess from photos.

We can help you assess, honestly and specifically:

  • which season fits your experience and goals;
  • whether a guided Rummu dive is realistic for your group;
  • what training — open water, pool or both — makes sense around your dates;
  • equipment questions for cold northern water;
  • local logistics: getting around, timing, backup plans.

How planning usually goes

An example, not a package: a pair of certified freedivers write to us in winter about a summer trip. We talk through their comfort depths and dates, agree that late summer fits best, sketch a plan of guided Rummu days with a pool session as the weather backup, and list the gear worth bringing. Closer to the dates, conditions get confirmed and the plan becomes bookings on our service site.

You will get a personal reply within two working days.
